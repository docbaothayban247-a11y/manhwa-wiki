📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

“Just give me a chance to love you.” Seo Yoon, a successful project planner, has a problem: the new employee Seo Dohyeon keeps invading her private space. He’s a man who doesn’t believe in love—yet he keeps talking to her about it. Afraid of the way he rushes in like a tidal wave, Seo Yoon decides it’s better to give him just one night and end things… “But what can I do? If you offer it, I’ll gladly take it.”

📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

Drenia of the demon world is an incubus who charges his life force through dream sex and provides it to a life force supply center for a living. However, due to poor performance, he is on the verge of being expelled from the incubus world. His colleague and succubus, Meginia, seduces Drenia, claiming that sex with a real human is an ultra-short, ultra-powerful life force charging method, and secretly sends him to the human world. Meanwhile, Park Jin-su, a promising rookie professional baseball player, ends his career due to a sudden serious injury and lives as the owner of a snack bar in front of Sowang Women’s University. He discovers Drenia collapsed in front of his shop, and as a result, they start living together, leading to a continuous stream of sexy and racy incidents…

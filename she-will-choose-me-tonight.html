📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

As a child, A-Rin is adopted by the wealthy Chairman Moon, but is shunned by his family. Although her adoptive mother, Anna, hates her guts, her snooty remarks only make A-Rin’s attachment to her stronger. When A-Rin grows up and discovers her ability to turn men into her minions by kissing them, she comes up with the devious plan to hold her brothers hostage and get what she wants from Anna. What results is a scandalous power struggle that threatens to topple the Moon Family’s legacy! Will A-Rin succeed in taking over the family that once treated her like dirt?

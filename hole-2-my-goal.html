📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

In “Hole 2 My Goal” Elliot has the bad luck of renting a place next to noisy neighbors. Just as he’s ready to confront them, he discovers they’re two lesbian girls — Chloe and Hazel. They promise to keep it down, but soon the same noise returns. Frustrated, Elliot decides on revenge! He wants to stop their adult games, but instead, he gets pulled into the hottest situation he’s ever imagined. If the girls expose him, he’s done for. But what if one of girls is bisexual and willing to keep his secret… for a price?

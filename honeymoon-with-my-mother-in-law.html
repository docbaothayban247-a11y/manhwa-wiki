📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

In “Honeymoon with my mother-in-law”, Oliver dreams of spending unforgettable days and nights with his wife, Chloe, after their wedding. When it turns out Chloe brought her mother with her, Oliver initially doesn’t see a problem. Until, through a series of coincidences, his honeymoon with Chloe turns into… a honeymoon with her mother! “Honeymoon with my mother-in-law” is a vacation that will stay in your memory forever and will forever remain your biggest secret.

📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

Eve Jenna, a poor noblewoman from the countryside, had only her beauty and practical mindset to rely on. In pursuit of financial stability, she became the fourth wife of Count Hound, hoping for security, if not love. Three years into their marriage, her seemingly stable life is shattered when her husband abruptly demands a divorce. That same night, Eve receives an unexpected visit from Azazel, her husband’s eldest son, who had always kept his distance. Without a trace of humor, he makes a shocking proposal: after her divorce, she should marry him. Stunned by the audacity of his offer, Eve dismisses it as mockery, but his next words leave her speechless—he’s always desired her. What began as a contract marriage soon spirals into a series of events Eve could never have foreseen.

📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

Duke Ron, who makes everyone nervous with his sensitive and prickly personality, is the only one among many maids who treats Joy specially. “It was only after you came to this mansion that I felt alive. I couldn’t help but love you, Joy…” Ron was saved by Joy, and he thought he would do anything for Joy. He didn’t know why Joy came to his duke’s mansion.

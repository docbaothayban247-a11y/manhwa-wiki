📜 Synopsis & Background Narrative

“I said I wanted to get tangled up with a handsome man, so what am I supposed to do if I become the handsome man? What kind of situation is this?” Born as the young lady of a noble family, Julia was raised to behave demurely and suppress all desires. Yet, perhaps due to the fervor of youth, she has a habit of comforting herself by imagining attractive men. Tonight, after comforting herself by imagining an article she’d glimpsed that morning, Julia fell into a deep sleep. When she opened her eyes to the morning sunlight, she discovered she was lying in an unfamiliar bed. And she was no longer ‘she’ but ‘he’. Moreover, ‘he’ was Elliot Legrize, the Duke, a handsome man who stole the hearts of every woman in the empire, yet treated all women coldly.
